## Findings

1. **Free-lane debuts split wide.** swe-1-7-medium lands mid-pack (14/23, 12/21 subset) with no zero face; glm-5-2 bottoms the table at 6/23 but holds two face-level scalps nobody else in the free tier has.
2. **glm-5-2 is the second model ever to pass ui-build — swe-2-high is the third, and the first to max it.** A-d9b79b46: astra (W36, low/medium/high) was the only passer before this week; glm-5-2 joined on 09-08; swe-2-high posted 12/12 on 09-10, repairing its own sibling's zero-delivery failure from the same week.
3. **Review face is model-specific, not family-specific — now proven within one family.** A-cdc3d11a: swe-medium ✓ 1.0 (first published pass anywhere) vs swe-2-high ✗ -3.0 vs glm ✗ -4. A-47eea242: glm ✓ 3 and swe-2-high ✓ 3 vs swe-medium ✗ 1. Two review cases, opposite splits between two same-vendor generations: single-model claims about "review capability" are noise until replicated, and upgrading a model does not inherit its review scalps.
4. **glm-5-2's delivery-contract failure is a model defect, not infrastructure.** Six papers (ops ×2, req-drift ×4) ended with full analyses in chat and **no deliverable file written**; two same-day makeup replays reproduced the exact mode. Classified as delivery failure （真答不出）， not `invalid_infrastructure`; marked ∅ with this footnote. A model that cannot honor "write report.md at path X" cannot be operated unattended regardless of its reasoning.
5. **The multi-dispatch crack does not replicate on swe-2.** A-569dbe0d: glm 3/10 (structural, seen on other lanes); swe-medium and swe-2-high both max 10/10.
6. **Vision stays a SWE-family scalp this week — and the ceiling keeps rising.** A-ea80d793: swe-1-7-medium ✓ 3.0, swe-2-high ✓ 3.0, **swe-2-medium ✓ 4.0 (best-ever)** — W37's only passes (luna/sol failed all five published bands; astra-medium passed it W36). The successor inherits the sense, and its cheaper band sees finest.
7. **No token telemetry on this lane.** The provider reports zero usage counters through ACP. Wall clock is the only speed proxy: swe-2-high 2.7 h, swe-2-medium 1.4 h, swe-1-7-medium 3.0 h, glm 2.3 h cumulative for 26 papers.
8. **swe-2-high nets +2 cases over its sibling — gains are delivery discipline, losses are review luck.** Fixed vs swe-medium: A-d9b79b46 (no-show → 12/12), A-0676097b (3/4 → 4/4), A-24bcf707. Lost: A-cdc3d11a (1.0 → -3.0), A-a5608487 (✓ → ✗). Verify face (0/3) is the untouched family ceiling; A-a317e74b regresses 13/15 → 7/15.
9. **Vendor launch-day claim vs this suite.** SWE-2 launched 2026-09-10 claiming frontier-parity scores at up to 70% lower cost. On this 23-case agentic suite the high band lands mid-pack of the current board (16/23; the week's high anchor elsewhere is 17/23): a real upgrade over swe-1-7-medium, not a frontier takeover. Cost-side, the UID is Free-marked on the subscription lane, so the price claim is untestable here.
10. **SWE-2's band curve is monotone — no sweet low band.** swe-2-medium 15/23 < swe-2-high 16/23, and the losses concentrate in heavy-judgment faces (A-442d4aab 7/7→1/7, A-a5608487, A-a317e74b 7/15→0/15). The same-week non-monotonic effort curve seen on deepseek-v4.1-flash elsewhere (none/high twin peaks, mid/xhigh dip) does not replicate here. The medium band is still a real Pareto point: ~3.4× faster than high for -1 case, and it posts the suite's **best-ever vision score (A-ea80d793 4.0)** plus a best-tying A-47eea242 4 — fast-scout work suits it.
11. **swe-2-max takes the suite-wide board — and the curve stays monotone to the top.** 18/23 (16/21) vs the previous 17/23 anchor: medium 15 < high 16 < max 18, no top-band backlash. Its signature is the **first-ever OPS 6/6 sweep**: A-a5608487 and A-8d4bc770 failed every other SWE entry on this lane; max clears both. The bill is wall clock: ~5 h cumulative (~4× the previous anchor's 1.16 h) for +1–2 cases, with a pathological tail on A-a317e74b (two 3600 s timeouts, case owed — every published entry fails that case, so the board is unaffected). Pick the band by deadline, not by hoping less thinking wins: medium for latency, high for daily driver, max for unattended overnight judgment.
12. **swe-2-low ties medium at the ladder bottom — same 15/23, opposite signature.** Low keeps the heavy-judgment cases medium collapses on (A-442d4aab 7/7 vs 1/7, A-a317e74b 7/15 vs 0/15) and posts the family's best A-cdc3d11a d2 (1.0, still a fail, 单次未复测） plus the fastest vision pass on record (A-ea80d793 3.0 in 175 s, vs medium's slower but higher 4.0). Its losses sit in ops archaeology (A-24bcf707, A-8d4bc770) — faces medium/high clear. The band curve is now low = medium < high < max: still no sweet cheap band (low does not beat medium), but the bottom rung is no longer a single profile — pick low for hard-analytic retention on a budget, medium for a fast clean sweep.

## Verdict

**swe-2-max is the new board top (18/23) and the band for unattended overnight judgment** — the only entry with an OPS 6/6 sweep, at ~4× the wall clock of the speed leaders. **swe-2-high is the daily all-rounder** (16/23). **swe-2-medium is the fast scout**: one case cheaper, ~3.4× faster than high, the suite's best-ever vision score. **swe-2-low ties it at 15/23 with the opposite signature** — the budget band that still holds hard-analytic cases (A-442d4aab 7/7, A-a317e74b 7/15) and posts the family's best review d2; slower and stall-prone on the heaviest ops papers. **swe-1-7-medium keeps a niche** wherever adversarial contract review is the job (its A-cdc3d11a scalp remains uninherited, even by its own successors). **glm-5-2 remains a single-strength specialist with an operational disqualifier.** Next on this lane: replicate max's OPS sweep and the ui-build/vision passes on a second night, re-run A-a317e74b for max with a longer cap, and complete the swe-1-7 (Max band) run (17/26 banked).
